Research experience

  • Apr – Sept 2026

    Quantum Measurement Lab, Imperial College London

    Master’s research project

    Combined heterodyne spectroscopy with analytical and numerical modelling of multimode Brillouin scattering in optical fibre, predicting the temperature dependence of neighbouring acoustic-mode spacings.

  • Jan – Apr 2025

    Atto@Kings, King’s College London

    Undergraduate research project

    Extended a numerical simulation framework to investigate electron trajectories and quantum-path phase coefficients in two-colour high-harmonic generation, exploring possible signatures of tunnelling without a barrier.

  • Jun – Aug 2023

    Levitated Nanophysics Group, King’s College London

    King’s Undergraduate Research Fellowship

    Investigated time-dependent trapping failure in a modified Paul trap, developed video particle-tracking software and used power spectral analysis to characterise the motion of levitated diamond particles.

Selected projects

  • Jul 2026

    Laser spectroscopy for magneto-optical trapping

  • Jan – Mar 2026

    Surface–GKP quantum error correction

    Reviewed GKP encoding, syndrome extraction, experimental implementations and concatenation with surface codes.

  • Oct – Nov 2025

    Ground-state energy estimation with SQD

    Applied sample-based quantum diagonalisation to estimate the electronic ground-state energy of a water molecule as part of a team project, and presented the results at an Institute of Physics event.

  • Sept 2023 – May 2024

    Cal Aerospace SAE, UC Berkeley

  • Jun – Aug 2020
