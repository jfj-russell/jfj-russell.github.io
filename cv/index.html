Research experience

  • Apr – Sept 2026

    Quantum Measurement Lab, Imperial College London

    Master’s research project

    Measured and resolved the multimode Brillouin spectrum of optical fibre using heterodyne spectroscopy. Developed analytical and numerical models of guided acoustic modes to predict the temperature dependence of their resonance spacings.

  • Jan – Apr 2025

    Atto@Kings, King’s College London

    Undergraduate research project

    Developed numerical simulations of electron trajectories and quantum-path phase coefficients in two-colour high-harmonic generation. Investigated their dependence on laser intensity and relative phase to identify possible signatures of tunnelling without a barrier.

  • Jun – Aug 2023

    Levitated Nanophysics Group, King’s College London

    King’s Undergraduate Research Fellowship

    Investigated the dynamics and long-term stability of levitated diamond particles in a modified Paul trap. Developed particle-tracking software and used power spectral analysis to characterise changes in their translational motion.

Selected projects

  • Jul 2026

    Laser spectroscopy for magneto-optical trapping

    Aligned free-space optics and recorded Doppler-free saturated-absorption spectra of rubidium, investigating laser-frequency drift in a system used for cooling-laser stabilisation.

  • Jan – Mar 2026

    Surface–GKP quantum error correction

    Reviewed the concatenation of GKP and surface codes for fault-tolerant quantum computing, examining syndrome extraction, decoding, finite squeezing and potential experimental implementations.

  • Oct – Nov 2025

    Ground-state energy estimation with SQD

    Applied sample-based quantum diagonalisation to estimate the ground-state energy of water, comparing hybrid quantum–classical results with classical reference calculations. Presented the project at an Institute of Physics event.

  • Sept 2023 – May 2024

    Cal Aerospace SAE, UC Berkeley

    Contributed to the structural design and analysis of a 15 ft competition aircraft, using SolidWorks and Ansys to investigate landing-gear performance under heavy landing loads.

  • Jun – Aug 2020

    In2scienceUK

    Investigated the optical properties of lead-halide perovskites and analysed spectral data to identify band-gap trends.
