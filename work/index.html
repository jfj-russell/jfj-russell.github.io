IMDEA Nanociencia

Time-resolved THz spectroscopy of semiconductors

Using ultrafast terahertz spectroscopy to follow how charge carriers move and relax in semiconductors, with a particular interest in photovoltaic and optoelectronic materials.

2026
Imperial College London

Multimode Brillouin scattering and its temperature dependence in optical fibre

Light travelling through an optical fibre can interact with several different acoustic modes, producing a series of closely spaced Brillouin resonances. Their relative spacing provides an interesting way to study how acoustic confinement changes with temperature.

2025
King’s College London

Quantum tunnelling without a barrier: phase-sensitive signatures in HHG

Can an electron tunnel even when there is no barrier to tunnel through? Two-colour laser fields offer a way to explore this question through the trajectories and quantum-path phases of electrons involved in high-harmonic generation.

2023
King’s College London

Dancing levitated diamonds in a modified Paul trap

Microscopic diamond particles suspended in a modified Paul trap can exhibit some rather unusual motion. By tracking the particles over time, we can explore their dynamics and the instabilities that eventually cause them to escape the trap.
