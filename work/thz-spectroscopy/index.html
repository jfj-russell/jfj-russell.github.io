Time-resolved THz spectroscopy of semiconductors

The project

The performance of semiconductor devices depends on how charge carriers move and recombine. Understanding these processes on ultrafast timescales is important for developing photovoltaic and optoelectronic materials.

I will be starting a PhD in the Nanostructured Photovoltaics group at IMDEA Nanociencia in Madrid, using time-resolved terahertz spectroscopy to investigate charge-carrier dynamics in semiconductors. This technique uses short optical pulses to excite a material and terahertz measurements to track changes in its photoconductivity.

This page will be updated as the research develops.
