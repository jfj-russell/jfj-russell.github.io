Time-resolved THz spectroscopy of semiconductors

The project

Semiconductors respond to light through the motion and interaction of electrons, holes and other short-lived excitations. These processes often unfold on femtosecond and picosecond timescales, making them difficult to observe with conventional electrical measurements.

My doctoral research at IMDEA Nanociencia will use time-resolved terahertz spectroscopy to follow these ultrafast dynamics. By exciting a material with a short optical pulse and probing its changing terahertz response, the project aims to understand the transport and recombination processes that shape photovoltaic and optoelectronic performance. This page will develop as the research begins.
